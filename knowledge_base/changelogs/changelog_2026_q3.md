# ShopEasy Policy Changelog — Q3 2026 (July onwards)

Recent changes to ShopEasy customer policies. Each entry lists the date the
change took effect. Where an entry conflicts with an older policy document,
the entry below is authoritative.

---

## 2026-07-15 — Electronics return window extended from 7 to 10 days

**Category:** returns

Effective July 15, 2026, the return window for electronics (mobiles, laptops,
tablets, cameras, and accessories) has been extended from 7 days to 10 days
from the date of delivery. This brings electronics in line with all other
returnable categories, which already have a 10-day window.

Note: older documents, including the Refund Policy dated June 1, 2026, still
state a 7-day window for electronics. This changelog entry supersedes them for
orders delivered on or after July 15, 2026. Large appliances remain at 7 days
(installation issues only), and non-returnable categories are unchanged.

---

## 2026-07-20 — Metro same-day delivery cutoff moved to 2 PM

**Category:** delivery

Effective July 20, 2026, orders placed before 2:00 PM (previously 12:00 noon)
in metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata,
Pune) qualify for same-day delivery on eligible items. Orders placed after
2:00 PM are delivered the next day. Non-metro delivery SLAs are unchanged.

---

## 2026-07-28 — Restocking fee waived for ShopEasy Plus members

**Category:** returns

Effective July 28, 2026, ShopEasy Plus members no longer pay the ₹75
restocking fee on change-of-mind returns for items priced above ₹999. The fee
continues to apply to non-member accounts. All other change-of-mind conditions
(original shipping charges not refunded, customer-arranged return shipping
where pickup is unavailable) still apply to all customers.

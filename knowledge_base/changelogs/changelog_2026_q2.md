# ShopEasy Policy Changelog — Q2 2026 (April–June)

Recent changes to ShopEasy customer policies. Each entry lists the date the
change took effect. Where an entry conflicts with an older policy document,
the entry below is authoritative.

---

## 2026-04-10 — COD order limit raised from ₹10,000 to ₹15,000

**Category:** payments

The maximum order value eligible for Cash on Delivery has been raised from
₹10,000 to ₹15,000 per order, effective April 10, 2026. All other COD rules
(pincode availability, ₹40 COD handling fee for orders under ₹499, maximum of
2 open COD orders per account) remain unchanged.

---

## 2026-05-02 — UPI refund processing time reduced to 3–5 business days

**Category:** payments

Refunds to UPI payment sources are now processed within 3–5 business days,
down from the earlier 5–7 business days, effective May 2, 2026. Refunds to
credit/debit cards continue to take 5–7 business days depending on the issuing
bank. Refunds for COD orders are issued to the customer's ShopEasy wallet or
bank account (via submitted details) within 7 business days.

---

## 2026-06-01 — New unified refund policy in effect

**Category:** returns

A revised refund policy applies to all orders placed on or after June 1, 2026.
Key points: 100% refund for defective, damaged, wrong, or not-as-described
items; change-of-mind returns over ₹999 attract a ₹75 restocking fee; seller
cancellations after payment earn a 100% refund plus a ₹100 compensation coupon.
For orders placed before June 1, 2026, the policy in effect at the time of
purchase applies. See the full Refund Policy document for details.
